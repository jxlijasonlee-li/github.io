About

01

I'm a researcher working at the intersection of computational modeling and experimental neuroscience. My recent work spans machine-learning reductions of biophysical neuron models, deep-learning pipelines for brain-wide neuronal mapping from light-sheet fluorescence microscopy, and the physics of how medical devices behave inside MRI fields. I trained in biomedical engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, spent time in Maurice Smith's lab at Harvard studying cognitive-assessment biomarkers, and I'm currently based at Yale, moving between the Laboratory of Physiology of Behavior, the McDougal Lab, and the Sensor Lab.

Research

03

Selected projects across computational modeling, neuroimaging, and biomedical device physics.

Machine-learning reduction of biophysical models for multiscale neural stimulation
10/2025 – 03/2026
McDougal Lab, Yale · Supervisor: Prof. Robert McDougal
  • Developed a machine-learning mechanism encoding neural dynamics to predict spiking behavior
  • Simulated neural dynamics and contributed to NEURON simulator and ModelDB development
Brain-wide 3D mapping of neuronal activity from light-sheet microscopy
10/2025 – 03/2026
Laboratory of Physiology of Behavior, Yale
  • Implemented a deep-learning pipeline for brain-wide 3D mapping of neuronal activity in LSFM datasets
Longitudinal stability of MMSE / MoCA cognitive assessments
07/2024 – 11/2024
Harvard Neuromotor Control Lab · Supervisor: Prof. Maurice Smith
  • Showed MMSE/MoCA diagnostic accuracy stays stable under repeated annual administration using the ADNI dataset
  • Ran a non-inferiority analysis showing AUC decline exceeded the nominal 1%-per-year threshold
  • Quantified item-level AUC decline to identify an optimal item subset
Dual-brain synchronization mechanisms of joint attention
Shanghai Jiao Tong University
Supervisor: Prof. Shanbao Tong
  • Used EEG hyperscanning with controlled variables to simulate real-life parent–child interaction
  • Built within-frequency dual-brain networks using phase-locking values and designed network thresholds
  • Identified the prefrontal cortex's key role in joint attention via enhanced network connectivity
Unsupervised, cross-modal segmentation of dynamic lung MRI
Shanghai Jiao Tong University
Supervisor: Prof. Puming Zhang
  • Used 4D dynamic MRI to capture the full respiratory cycle
  • Aligned cross-modal representations through unsupervised domain adaptation
  • Improved Hausdorff distance, sensitivity, and specificity by 5–15% over baseline
Unsupervised segmentation of brain stroke lesions
Shanghai Institute of Medical Device Testing
Supervisor: Dr. Xun Liu
  • Leveraged f-AnoGAN and GMVAE models to segment stroke lesions from MRI
  • Improved SSIM by 20% over baseline
Temperature-rise mechanisms of medical devices in gradient magnetic fields
Shanghai Institute of Medical Device Testing
Supervisor: Prof. Puming Zhang
  • Examined how initial temperature, gel thickness, and waveform shape drive heating in the MR environment
  • Showed sine-wave fields heat more than triangular waves at the same switching rate
  • Derived and experimentally validated a lumped-parameter thermal model
